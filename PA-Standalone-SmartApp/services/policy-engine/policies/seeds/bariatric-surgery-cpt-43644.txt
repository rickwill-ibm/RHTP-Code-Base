MEDICAL POLICY: Bariatric Surgery (Surgical Treatment of Morbid Obesity)
Policy ID: bariatric-surgery-cpt-43644
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Payer: Blue Cross Prior Authorization
Governed CPT Codes: 43644, 43645, 43770, 43771, 43772, 43773, 43774, 43775

OVERVIEW
Prior authorization is required for all bariatric surgical procedures. The following criteria must be met for coverage of bariatric surgery.

MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA

All of the following criteria (I through III) must be met:

I. Primary Obesity Diagnosis
The member must have a documented diagnosis of morbid (severe) obesity with ICD-10-CM code E66.01 (Morbid (severe) obesity due to excess calories) OR E66.09 (Other obesity due to excess calories) OR E66.1 (Drug-induced obesity) on the problem list, confirmed by the treating physician.

II. Body Mass Index (BMI) Requirement
The member must have a documented BMI of 35 kg/m² or greater within the past 12 months, measured and recorded as a vital sign or observation. The BMI must be supported by current weight and height measurements.
- BMI >= 35 with at least one qualifying comorbidity (see Criterion III), OR
- BMI >= 40 without comorbidity requirement

BMI is recorded using LOINC code 39156-5. Weight is recorded using LOINC code 29463-7. Height is recorded using LOINC code 8302-2.

III. Qualifying Comorbidity
At least one of the following obesity-related comorbid conditions must be documented as an active diagnosis:

A. Type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10: E11.0, E11.1, E11.2, E11.3, E11.4, E11.5, E11.6, E11.7, E11.8, E11.9, E11.65)
B. Hypertension (ICD-10: I10, I11, I12, I13)
C. Obstructive sleep apnea (ICD-10: G47.33, G47.30, G47.31, G47.39)
D. Obesity hypoventilation syndrome (ICD-10: E66.2)
E. Pulmonary hypertension (ICD-10: I27.0, I27.20, I27.21, I27.22, I27.23, I27.24, I27.29)
F. Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease / NASH (ICD-10: K75.81, K76.0)
G. Gastroesophageal reflux disease (ICD-10: K21.0, K21.9)
H. Osteoarthritis of weight-bearing joints (ICD-10: M16.0, M16.1, M17.0, M17.1, M17.9)
I. Dyslipidemia / hyperlipidemia (ICD-10: E78.00, E78.01, E78.1, E78.2, E78.3, E78.4, E78.5)
J. Polycystic ovary syndrome (ICD-10: E28.2)
K. Stress urinary incontinence (ICD-10: N39.3)
L. Venous stasis disease (ICD-10: I87.2, I87.009, I87.019)
M. Pseudotumor cerebri / idiopathic intracranial hypertension (ICD-10: G93.2)

IV. Conservative Treatment Attempts (Supportive — not required for all plans)
Documentation that the member has attempted and failed a supervised medical weight management program for at least 6 months in the preceding 2 years. This may include physician-supervised diet programs, behavioral therapy, or pharmacotherapy.

V. Surgical Readiness
A. The member must be 18 years of age or older.
B. The member must have undergone a pre-surgical psychological evaluation.
C. No active substance use disorder.
D. No uncontrolled psychiatric illness that would impair ability to comply with post-operative requirements.

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- Current height and weight with calculated BMI
- Operative note from bariatric surgeon
- Letter of medical necessity from bariatric surgeon
- Records of comorbid conditions with treatment history
- Records of prior weight loss attempts (if applicable)
- Psychological evaluation report

EXCLUSIONS
- Revision bariatric surgery requires separate review
- Members under 18 require additional pediatric criteria
- Experimental procedures are not covered
